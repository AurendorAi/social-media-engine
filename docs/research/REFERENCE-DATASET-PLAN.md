# Study the owner-provided design dataset

Updated 2026-10-15 following the owner's correction.

The owner will prepare and supply the professional-design dataset. Do not find, download,
adopt or substitute a third-party dataset. The earlier Crello/agency seed proposal is
withdrawn. No examples from that proposal were downloaded or admitted as training material.
Prior source research remains in Git history; it is not the active collection plan.

## Intake when the owner supplies it

Record the supplied folder/archive, complete designs and all slides, source/creator
information where available, usage permissions, brand/campaign groupings and the owner's
examples of strong and weak work. Preserve originals. Identify missing slides, duplicates,
unreadable previews and unclear permissions before counting accepted study records.
Do not invent dataset size, annotations, designer intent or a professional approval.

Study actual complete exports at readable resolution. Separate observable composition,
photography, typography, materials, hierarchy and narrative from interpretation. Explain
why a detail belongs to the subject and how its principle can transfer to another brand.
Attach each proposed rule to specific examples and counterexamples. Keep brand/creator/
campaign holdouts for later testing rather than memorizing variations of the same design.

## Production assets are a different library

The bundled `assets/resource-libraries.json` is a directory of places to find free fonts,
photographs, textures, patterns, existing graphics and other production ingredients.
It is not a dataset, a corpus to study, or permission to train on its assets. Searching
those sources is authorized for composing an actual requested design. It does not authorize
bulk collection, asset redistribution or replacing the owner's study material.

## Validation

Use the owner's quality references and feedback to calibrate evaluations before assigning
professional labels. A readable layout or a numeric agent score is not design acceptance.
The owner rejected the previous vector-only samples; preserve those failures and retire
that production approach. New vector creation is prohibited. Use sourced assets, permitted
raster generation/editing and actual composition tools, with real final-output inspection.

Any later model comparisons must exercise the intended production workflow and available
tools, with matched approved assets, not a vector-only substitute. Record all failures,
provider costs and limitations. No promise of equal quality from every model follows from
adding references or instructions; publishing still requires actual owner approval.
